# 📺 OMG Premium TV 8.5.1 — everything new since 8.2

## 📡 Xtream Codes accounts
- **New source**: besides M3U playlists you can use an **Xtream Codes account** directly (server, username, password). Pasting the full `get.php` link also works.
- **Account check** and **subscription expiry** in the configuration: status, expiry date with days left and connections, with a warning when 15 days or fewer remain.
- The **provider's TV guide** turns on by itself if you don't set an EPG.
- Credentials never appear in addon responses or logs.
- **Streams through the proxy now work**: many Xtream panels reject (error 401) requests that look like a browser. The proxy, EasyProxy or MediaFlow, now presents itself as an IPTV player, even with WARP on. `.mp4` movies use EasyProxy's compatible endpoint.
- No more pre-check of Xtream streams, which used up one of the subscription's connections.

## 🎬 Movies and TV series (Xtream)
- **"Include movies and TV series" option**: Movies and Series catalogs with search, detail pages with plot, cast and episodes grouped by season.
- **IMDb linking** with your free TMDB key: the stream also shows up when you open the movie or episode from the app's own detail pages. Matches are stored in your configuration, and later updates only look up new titles.
- **Provider categories and subcategories** as filters (`IT | Action` → `IT › Action`) and **categories to hide** with a checkbox, for example adult content or languages you don't need.
- **TMDB posters** in your language and for your country, with a backdrop on the detail page. Provider covers that don't load are dropped. Titles with no image get a placeholder with the title: red for movies, blue for series.
- **More accurate search**: titles cleaned of prefixes, years and tags (`IT - The Godfather (1972)` → `The Godfather`), and remakes sharing a title are not mixed up.
- Titles available in several copies (HD, 4K…) appear once, with all their streams.
- Movies and series are updated **together with the playlist**, including with the "Reload playlist" channel.

## 📺 Channels
- **No more mixed-up channels**: some playlists reuse one id for different channels, so every Sky channel ended up inside Sky Cinema Uno. Now every channel goes back to its own place with its own TV guide.
- **One channel, every quality**: SD, HD, FHD and 4K of the same channel become a single channel, with a stream for each version. This also works with playlists that have no `tvg-id`.
- **Clean names**: the channel list no longer shows HD, FHD, SD or 4K ("La 7 HD" → "La 7"), which stay on the streams, nor decorative dashes (`-----RAI 1-----` → `RAI 1`). "Rai 4K" stays "Rai 4K".
- Channels saved in favourites or "continue watching" are still found even if the playlist slightly renames them.

## ▶️ Streams and proxy
- Streams through the proxy are no longer dropped when the quick check fails. Verified streams 🌐 come first, then **"not verified"** ones ⚠️, still through the proxy: with "Proxy only" your IP stays hidden.
- **New "Disabled" choice** for the proxy type: URL and password stay saved so you can turn it back on whenever you like.
- With several configurations installed, the ones that don't have a channel no longer show an error.

## 🧭 Interface
- Every configuration is named **`OMG+ TV - <name>`**: addon and catalogs stay consistent even with several configurations installed.
- Simpler setup: EPG on by default, language and name right under the country, a note about the provider's TV guide for Xtream accounts.
- Addon description in the configuration's language (Italian, English, Spanish, French).
- Texts that fit every app (Stremio, Nuvio…) and every source (M3U and Xtream). The service channel is called **"Reload playlist"**.
- Placeholders for channels, movies and series use the Montserrat font and keep "+" and accented letters ("Rai 1 +1").

## ⬆️ How to update
1. Update the addon: `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`.
2. In each configuration open **⚙️ → Reload playlist**, or wait for the automatic refresh.
3. **Reinstall the addon in Stremio or Nuvio with the same URL** to get the updated name, description and filters.
4. With movies and series on, titles are looked up on TMDB again at first start to get the new posters. Large catalogs take a few minutes.
